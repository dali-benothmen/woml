# WOML Extension Support

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dali-benothmen/woml/issues) for bug
reports and feature requests.

For highlighting problems, include:

- a minimal `.woml` example;
- your Visual Studio Code version;
- the WOML extension version;
- your active color theme;
- your active file-icon theme; and
- a screenshot when the visual result matters.

Do not include workflow secrets, tokens, credentials, or private payloads in an
issue.
